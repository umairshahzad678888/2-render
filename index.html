Custom Fursuit Head: Built to Your Reference Sheet

A custom fursuit head is not a mask off a shelf. It is shaped, furred, and fitted for one character and one head.

Every custom head starts with a reference sheet. The clearer the reference, the more accurate the quote and the better the final match to your character.

Made to measurement

A custom head is built to your head size so it sits steady and comfortable through a long day. The base is hand carved foam or a 3D printed resin shell, shaped to the character.

Vision and comfort

High vision mesh eyes keep your sight clear while the eye shape stays true to the design. Real ventilation is what makes a head wearable at a con, not just in a photo.
